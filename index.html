This week at a glance

  • How things are going. The free tour campaign brought 9 leads into the CRM, up from 6 the week before, and cost per lead fell to $25.56 from $36.60 on $230.02 in spend. Nine leads is a small count, so it is too early to call a trend.
  • What to look into. Cost per lead of $25.56 is still well above the usual $10.41. Nearly all spend now goes to the Flex ad, $194.07 of $230.02, at $27.72 per lead, and link clicks fell to 122 from 149.
  • What is next. We recommend testing one new free tour ad with a different opening line, launched as a new ad rather than an edit, so the account has a second ad that can earn spend.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The Flex free tour ad brought 7 leads at $27.72, and Control Ad 2 brought 2 at $17.92. Both use the same words: they open 'Lombard families have been walking through our doors for over 40 years. Come see why,' list two heated pools, eight racquetball courts and a sauna, and offer one free tour: 'No membership required. Nothing to sign.' We recommend testing a new ad that opens with 'No membership required. Nothing to sign,' since every live ad says the same thing.
