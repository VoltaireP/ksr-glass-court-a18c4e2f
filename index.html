Nov 6 to Nov 12. Arrows compare with the week before (Oct 30 to Nov 5).

This week at a glance

  • How things are going. CRM leads were 17 at $13.18 each, down from 20 at $10.89 the week before, on $223.99 of spend.
  • What to look into. Meta and the CRM both counted 17 leads, so tracking looks normal. The long free tour video took $198.42 of the $223.99 spent, and the control ad from Nov 3 got $5.40 and no leads, so results still rest on one ad.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a new long video with a different opening next to the current one, since the other ads are not earning spend against it and cost per lead has risen two weeks in a row.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The long free tour video brought 15 leads at $13.23. It opens 'Lombard families have been walking through our doors for nearly 50 years. Come see why,' lists two heated pools, eight racquetball courts and a sauna, and offers one free tour with nothing to sign. The vertical cut and one more version of the same copy brought 1 lead each. Worth testing a long video that opens on the pools or the sauna, since one opening carries nearly all the leads and its cost is rising.
