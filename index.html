This week at a glance

  • How things are going. CRM leads were 10 at $22.18 each, down from 17 at $13.20 the week before, on $221.85 of spend.
  • What to look into. One control ad spent $84.92 for 1 lead, while the long free tour video brought 10 leads at $12.40 on $124.01. Meta counted 11 leads and the CRM 10, so tracking looks normal.
  • What is next. We recommend testing a new cut of the long video with a different opening in place of the control ad, since the long video is the only ad bringing leads at a steady cost.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The long free tour video brought 10 leads at $12.40. It opens 'Lombard families have been walking through our doors for nearly 50 years. Come see why,' lists two heated pools, eight racquetball courts and a sauna, and offers one free tour with nothing to sign. The control ad uses the same copy with the headline 'Tour Glass Court FREE' and brought 1 lead at $84.92, so the video likely does the work. Worth testing a long video that opens on the pools or the sauna.
