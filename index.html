This week at a glance

  • How things are going. The free tour campaign brought 6 leads into the CRM, down from 18 the week before, and cost per lead rose to $36.60 from $10.41 on $219.61 in spend. Six leads is a small count, so it is too early to call this a trend.
  • What to look into. Link clicks held steady at 149 against 147 the week before, but leads fell from 18 to 6. All three free tour ads were edited on Oct 10 and went back through Meta's review, which likely reset their delivery, so it is worth confirming the lead form still works.
  • What is next. We recommend leaving all three ads unedited for one full week so they can settle. When the copy needs to change, we recommend launching it as a new ad rather than editing a live one.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The Flex free tour ad brought 5 of the 6 leads at $33.04 each, and Control Ad 2 brought 1. All three ads use the same words: they open 'Lombard families have been walking through our doors for over 40 years. Come see why,' list two heated pools, eight racquetball courts and a sauna, and offer one free tour: 'No membership required. Nothing to sign.' We recommend testing a new ad that opens with 'No membership required. Nothing to sign,' since every live ad says the same thing.
