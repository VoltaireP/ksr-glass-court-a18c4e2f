This week at a glance

  • How things are going. CRM leads were 20 at $10.88 each, down from 26 at $8.80 the week before, on $217.67 of spend.
  • What to look into. Meta counted 21 leads and the CRM 20, a normal gap. Nearly all the spend, $202.53 of $217.67, went to the long free tour video, so the results rest on one ad.
  • What is next. We recommend giving the new free tour control ads a full week of spend before judging them against the long video's $11.25 per lead.
  • Creative ideas to consider. The long free tour video brought 18 leads at $11.25 and the vertical video 2 at $5.38. Both open 'Lombard families have been walking through our doors for nearly 50 years. Come see why,' list two heated pools, eight racquetball courts and a sauna, and offer one free tour with no membership and nothing to sign. Worth testing a long video that opens on the pools or the sauna instead, since one opening now carries nearly all the leads.
